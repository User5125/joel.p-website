About

I've spent my career on the other end of the phone, chat window, or email thread from someone who needs help — and I've learned that most people don't want a fast answer, they want the right one. That means listening properly, checking the details, and following up until things are actually fixed.

Most of my experience has been supporting Bolt's UK riders and drivers through Teleperformance Kenya, handling everything from billing questions to account verification and compliance documentation. I keep detailed records of every interaction, escalate the cases that need it, and I've trained several new teammates on how to do the same.

Outside of the day-to-day queue, I care about getting the small things right: accurate notes, a clear explanation, and a customer who isn't left wondering what happens next.
